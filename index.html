Wireless Networks · WSN/IoT · AI for Communications · Network Optimization & Resource Allocation · Distributed/Federated Learning · Energy-Efficient Communication Systems

I am an Electronics and Communication Engineering graduate from Khulna University of Engineering & Technology (KUET), with a primary research interest in wireless sensor networks and their broader connection to Internet of Things and intelligent wireless systems. My work has explored learning-based approaches to network optimization, resource allocation, energy management and distributed decision-making, with particular attention to the communication and resource constraints of networked sensing systems.

My undergraduate research focused on energy-efficient WSN operation through event-driven hierarchical reinforcement learning, while my other research has involved federated and graph-based learning for sensor-network applications, communication-efficient knowledge transfer and intelligent sensing. More broadly, I am interested in developing intelligent wireless and IoT systems in which learning, communication efficiency, energy consumption and system-level constraints are considered together.
