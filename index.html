Undergraduate thesis

A closer look at my thesis

Spatially-Constrained Event-Driven Hierarchical Deep Reinforcement Learning (SCED-HRL) Framework for Energy Optimization in Wireless Sensor Networks

B.Sc. thesis · Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering, KUET

SCED-HRL

Approach and evaluation

  • Built an event-driven PPO strategic layer with DDQN-based tactical agents for energy-aware WSN control.
  • Used spatial energy budgeting and feasibility constraints to manage energy consumption across sensor regions.
  • Evaluated with Intel Berkeley sensor data and synthetic traffic against baseline control strategies, considering lifetime, energy fairness, latency, and packet delivery.
